# S47: Hamdan bin Mohammed launches Dubai Digital Strategy (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

* Launch of the Dubai Digital Strategy on 21 June 2023 by Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed at Digital Dubai; implemented by Digital Dubai with government and private partners.
* Seven pillars: digital city, digital economy, data and statistics, digital talent, digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital competitiveness.
* Targets: increase the positive impact of Digital Wellbeing by 90%; launch 50 digital city experiences; equip over 50,000 professionals with advanced digital qualifications; top rankings in the UN Local Online Service Index.
* No deadline is stated for any target, no baseline for digital wellbeing, and no rank value for top rankings.
