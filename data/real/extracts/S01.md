# S01 — UAE Cabinet meeting statement, 23 April 2026 (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction of the official UAE Media Office page. These are notes,
not the source text. Verify against the URL in `sources.json`.

- Date: Thursday 23 April 2026, after a UAE Cabinet meeting chaired by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.
- Target: transform 50% of UAE federal government sectors, services and operations to autonomous agentic AI.
- Timeframe: two years. No start date is given for the two years.
- Rollout: phased across ministries and federal entities, based on performance and impact assessments, followed by wider deployment.
- Assessment of ministers, directors general and entity heads covers four things: ability to adopt the transformation; speed in implementing the new standards; understanding of the new technological reality; mastery of AI tools for creating new work mechanisms.
- A dedicated taskforce is overseen by Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan and chaired by Mohammad Abdullah Al Gergawi, Minister of Cabinet Affairs. No other members are named.
- Federal employees will receive continuous training in generative AI and related tools.
- Policies and resolutions approved at the meeting: UAE Code for Government Services and Zero Bureaucracy; government services digital records policy; government services data sharing policy (collect data once, use it securely across entities); federal guide for aligning digital government projects with national priorities; Geographical Indication framework; Unified Digital Postal Addressing System.
- Not present in this statement: any figure of 150 million data points, any figure of 38 entities, any figure of 70%.
