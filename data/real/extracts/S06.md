# S06 — Gulf News: UAE targets 50% of government services on agentic AI (updated 17 August 2026) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction. Secondary reporting. Notes, not source text.

- Higher Committee for Agentic AI organised the "Agentic AI and Data Technology Track" workshop with government leaders, Chief AI Officers and representatives of government entities.
- The track is chaired by Omar Sultan Al Olama, Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence, Digital Economy and Remote Work Applications.
- Target as reported: transform 50% of government sectors, operations and services into agentic AI-powered models within two years.
- Five tracks under the UAE Government 4.0 journey: operations and institutional support; strategic government work; government services; technology and data; capacity building and training.
- Federal Agentic AI Technology Platform (FedAI): shared technology infrastructure for federal entities to build and adopt agentic AI solutions. National AI Gateway: a single secure access point to multiple AI platforms; commercial and open-source models hosted in sovereign infrastructure.
- Classification and assessment: systems will be classified and assessed through a self-assessment mechanism based on technical and operational criteria.
- Requirements discussed: data governance, infrastructure, platforms, technical needs, integration of government systems. More than 10 technology vendors were showcased.
