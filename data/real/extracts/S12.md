# S12 — UAE National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031 (ai.gov.ae) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Eight strategic objectives: build a reputation as an AI destination; increase competitive assets in priority sectors through AI; develop a fertile ecosystem for AI; adopt AI across customer services to improve lives and government; attract and train talent for future AI-enabled jobs; bring world-leading research capability to target industries; provide the data and infrastructure to become an AI test bed; ensure strong AI governance and effective regulation.
- Economic value: up to AED 335 billion of potential AI-driven gains.
- Government and workforce: 100% of senior government leadership (Director-General, Ministerial and Senior-Ministerial levels) to be trained in AI; skills training for 60% of the workforce with low digital skills.
- The document sets no explicit target for government performance or the percentage of government services to be AI-enabled; its only government-specific numeric target is the senior leadership training goal.
