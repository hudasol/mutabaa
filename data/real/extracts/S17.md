# S17: Cabinet meeting, digital economy strategy and Council (April 2022) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- The Cabinet adopted the UAE Digital Economy Strategy.
- Goal: digital economy share of GDP to rise from 9.7% to 19.4% within ten years.
- The strategy contains more than 30 initiatives and programmes covering 6 sectors and 5 new areas of growth.
- It aims to make the UAE a regional and global digital economy hub and to set a unified way to measure growth.
- The Cabinet approved the establishment of the UAE Council for Digital Economy, chaired by Omar bin Sultan Al Olama, Minister of State for AI, Digital Economy and Teleworking Applications.
- The Council is to double the contribution of the digital economy to GDP in the year 2031.
- The quoted statement from Sheikh Mohammed gives a goal of 20 percent, while the text gives 19.4%; the page does not explain the difference or whether 20 percent is a share or an increase.
- Starting year for the ten years is not stated. No named measurement report is given.
