# S38: Dubai Self-Driving Transport Strategy announcement (RTA) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

* Announcement of the Dubai Self-Driving Transport Strategy by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.
* Target: 25 per cent of all transportation trips in Dubai to be smart and driverless by 2030.
* Stated economic returns: up to Dh22 billion a year in revenues and savings, built from cost savings, lower pollution, efficiency gains and fewer accidents.
* Other stated returns include a 13% rise in individual productivity and up to 396 million hours of travel time saved yearly.
* Owner: the Roads and Transport Authority and Dubai Future Foundation in a joint venture.
* The page does not define smart or driverless, gives no baseline, and names no measurement report.
