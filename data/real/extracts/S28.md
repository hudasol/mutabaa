# S28: Integrated Transport Centre and Lumo to implement first autonomous public transport bus trial in MENA (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Dated 19 June 2026; led by the Integrated Transport Centre (Abu Dhabi Mobility) with support from the Smart and Autonomous Systems Council and the company Lumo.
- Plans the first autonomous public transport bus trial in the MENA region; pilots and technical studies are to be explored.
- Restates that under the Abu Dhabi Smart and Autonomous Mobility Strategy, smart and autonomous modes are to reach 25% of total trips by 2040.
- No deadline is given for the bus trial, pilots or studies.
