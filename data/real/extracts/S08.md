# S08 — AI News: Abu Dhabi AI training programme for government employees (8 October 2026) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction. Secondary reporting of a DGE announcement. Notes, not source text.

- Published 8 October 2026; announced by DGE on 7 October 2026 at Ai Everything Abu Dhabi.
- Programme: AiNative, AI training for government staff, with a separate track for senior officials. OpenAI, Microsoft and MBZUAI helped develop the curriculum.
- Official target reported: 20,000 employees trained by end of 2026.
- A separate figure from DGE's Faisal Al Hawi: about 23,000 employees by early 2027.
- Public access: phased rollout; no general availability date announced.
- Also restated: more than 200 AI-powered solutions under the Digital Strategy 2025-2027; more than 1,000 AI agents planned through the AI Factory; sovereign cloud expected to support more than 11 million daily digital interactions.
- Owner: Department of Government Enablement (DGE).
