# S13 — Abu Dhabi Media Office: DGE launches AiNative training programme (7 October 2026) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-10 via web-fetch extraction of the official Abu Dhabi Media Office page. Notes, not source text.

- Date: 7 October 2026.
- Target: upskill 20,000 government employees by the end of the year. The page does not tie "the year" to a specific year.
- Wider goal restated: Abu Dhabi to become the world's first AI-native government by 2027.
- Owner: Department of Government Enablement (DGE). Curriculum shaped in collaboration with OpenAI, Microsoft and MBZUAI; the programme is described as open and technology-agnostic.
- Tracks: foundation learning journeys; an executive programme for department leaders; function-specific certifications to follow in later phases; a public journey through the Tomouh app.
- No count of employees already trained is given. An initial pilot within government is underway; the public programme comes later.
- Not stated: start and end dates of the pilot, budget, completion targets.
