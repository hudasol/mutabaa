# S14 — Abu Dhabi Media Office: DGE, Microsoft and Core42 sovereign cloud agreement (18 March 2025) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-10 via web-fetch extraction of the official Abu Dhabi Media Office page. Notes, not source text.

- Date: 18 March 2025.
- DGE, on behalf of the Abu Dhabi Government, signed a multi-year agreement with Microsoft and Core42 (a G42 company) to implement a sovereign cloud system.
- Capacity: a unified sovereign cloud environment that can handle more than 11 million daily digital interactions between government entities, citizens, residents and businesses.
- Adoption share: not stated. The page does not say what share of government services or workloads is on the sovereign cloud.
- Timeline: described only as multi-year; no end date or phases.
- Restated targets: automate 100 percent of government processes; AED 13 billion behind the Digital Strategy 2025-2027; over 200 AI-driven solutions; world's first fully AI-native government by 2027.
- Not stated: contract value, number of entities or services migrated, basis for the 11 million figure.
