# S05 — DGE: Microsoft Frontier Employee Programme (6 July 2026) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction of the DGE page. Notes, not source text.

- Date: 6 July 2026.
- 26,000 civil servants newly given Microsoft 365 Copilot across 27 government entities; 9,000 existing licences; total 35,000.
- Programme: Frontier Employee Programme; Copilot as the standard AI productivity platform for Abu Dhabi Government.
- Advanced Data Residency licences keep AI processing within UAE borders.
- Includes AI training and certification, an AI Adoption and Enablement framework (phased rollout, change management), security and readiness assessments, and an AI Factory capability for developing and scaling AI use cases and agents.
- Stated measurable targets: hundreds of AI use cases across the public sector; more than 1,000 AI agents across the public sector (document processing, constituent queries, policy analysis).
- The sovereign cloud, agreed in March 2025, is designed to process more than 11 million daily digital interactions.
- The Government Security Operations Centre supports about 60,000 users. TAMM delivers more than 1,150 services on one platform.
- Owners: Department of Government Enablement (DGE) leads; Microsoft is technology partner; Core42 is party to the 2025 sovereign cloud agreement.
- The article gives no progress metrics toward the AI-native 2027 goal.
