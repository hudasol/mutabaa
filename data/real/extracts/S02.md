# S02 — Abu Dhabi Government launches Digital Strategy 2025-2027 (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction of the Abu Dhabi Media Office page. Notes, not source text.

- Launch date: 21 January 2025.
- Investment: AED 13 billion through 2025-2027.
- Sovereign cloud: target of full adoption for government operations.
- Digitisation and automation: target of 100% of government processes.
- AI solutions: over 200 across government services.
- GDP contribution: expected to exceed AED 24 billion by 2027.
- Employment: more than 5,000 jobs, supporting Emiratisation.
- A unified digital enterprise resource planning platform to streamline processes.
- AI for All: a programme to train and empower citizens in AI applications.
- Owner: Department of Government Enablement (DGE), implemented with Abu Dhabi government entities.
- Formal pillars are not named in the article; it describes focus areas: digital infrastructure, digitisation and automation, the unified ERP platform, AI for All, AI solutions across services, cybersecurity frameworks.
