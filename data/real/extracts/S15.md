# S15: The UAE Digital Government Strategy 2025 (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- The page sets strategic KPIs with a 2025 target year.
- Citizen satisfaction with digital government services: 90% very satisfied. Business satisfaction is listed with no target figure.
- Share of services listed on the unified digital platform: 90%.
- Share of services digitised end to end: 100%.
- Services needing personal verification, and services needing a manual signature, are each to have a digital option in 100% of cases.
- Federal workforce trained on digital government capabilities and standards: 100%. Basic digital knowledge: 100%.
- Federal workforce trained in modern technologies such as blockchain and AI: 10%.
- Transactions completed digitally end to end: 85%.
- Maturity model has five levels and eight dimensions. Target: 100% of federal and local entities at very high maturity by 2025.
- The roadmap has six pillars and "64 national digital enablers" (the pillar counts shown add up to 63, a discrepancy on the page).
- Six objectives are listed without figures. Eight strategy dimensions are named (for example Digital by design, Data-driven, Open by default).
- No owner is named for the strategy as a whole. No baselines are given for any KPI. No measurement report is named.
- Related items on the page: the Government Services Strategy figures (see F05) and local strategies such as the Ajman Digital Masterplan (not federal).
