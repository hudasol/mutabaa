# S27: ITC expands operation of autonomous taxis to Al Reem and Al Maryah Islands (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Dated 29 July 2025; Integrated Transport Centre (Abu Dhabi Mobility) works with WeRide, Uber and the local operator Tawasul Transport.
- Announces the expansion of autonomous taxi service to Al Reem and Al Maryah Islands.
- States the emirate aims for a quarter of trips to use smart transport by 2040.
- Further plans: more areas of Abu Dhabi Island and a path to commercial operation, both without dates.
- Fleet size and distance travelled are results and are not recorded here.
