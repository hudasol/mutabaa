# S18: National Agenda (UAE Vision 2021) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- The seven year National Agenda was launched by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid in 2014 to support UAE Vision 2021.
- It has 52 national key performance indicators across nine sectors, set to be achieved by 2021.
- Development involved over 300 officials from 90 federal and local government entities.
- Six national priorities are named, including a competitive knowledge economy and a first-rate education system.
- Indicators are periodically monitored by government leadership; Government Accelerators were launched by the Cabinet in October 2016 as a mechanism to boost achievement.
- The page lists no individual indicator values, baselines or owners, and no digital, internet or smart service figures.
