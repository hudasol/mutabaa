# S21: Evaluation of 1,300 digital government services (June 2021) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Sheikh Mohammed directed federal ministries and departments to evaluate more than 1,300 digital and smart services.
- The assessment is to be done within three months and looks at customer experience through digital channels.
- A public ranking of the five best and worst government entities is to be announced on 4th September, 2021; scoring method not given and "five best and worst" is ambiguous.
- Evaluation criteria listed: ease of service and information, service experience, fewer application steps, easy fee payment, accuracy, request tracking, speed. No numeric scoring.
- The article repeats strategy figures: more than 28 initiatives by 2023, 90 percent of services on one platform by 2022, satisfaction above 90 percent.
- Data to be requested only once, with a common data sharing platform.
