# S42: Smart Dubai: paperless programme update (target text only) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

* Smart Dubai release of 1 March 2021 on the paperless programme. Only target wording is recorded.
* Each entity aimed to cut paper use by half within its first six months of joining.
* Entities continue toward 100% digitization by the end of 2021.
* After 12/12/2021 no Dubai Government entity will issue internal or external paper documents.
* Ultimate goal: make the Dubai Government the world's first paperless government.
* Government to individual services are to be offered through one platform, the DubaiNow application.
