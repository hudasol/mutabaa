# S40: Dubai Paperless Strategy: 14 Dubai Government entities (progress announcement, target text only) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

* Smart Dubai announcement of 21 April 2020 about the Dubai Paperless Strategy, launched in February 2018 by Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed. Only target wording is recorded.
* Overall goal: eliminate the use of paper from all internal operations and customer facing services.
* Final deadline: entities stop paper use for internal and external transactions after 2021.
* Phase deadlines: group three cuts paper consumption by 50% by the end of April 2020; group four by November 2020; group five by January 2021.
* Group three has nine named entities, including Dubai Culture and the Department of Finance Dubai.
* A 100% Paperless Stamp, launched March 2020, is awarded to entities that fully implement the strategy.
* No numeric target for services, sheets of paper or cost is stated.
