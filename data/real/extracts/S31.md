# S31: Department of Government Enablement Abu Dhabi launches TAMM 3.0 (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Dated 15 October 2024; Department of Government Enablement (DGE) and the TAMM Programme.
- TAMM 3.0 is described as bringing more than 800 government services onto one platform.
- The TAMM Nexus method is described as offering a potential 70-80% improvement in speed, accuracy and quality (a projection).
- Links the work to the goal of an AI-native government and to the UAE National AI Strategy 2031.
- No deadline for further rollout is given.
