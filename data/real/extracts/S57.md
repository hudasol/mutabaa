# S57: Ajman Digital Transformation Master Plan 2017-2022 (Arabic page) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Arabic page for the Ajman digital transformation plan covering 2017 to 2022.
- Targets stated: digital transformation of 100 percent of government services by end of 2018; digital transformation of all shared government services by end of 2018.
- Full adoption of 80 percent of digital services by end of 2021.
- Digital transformation of 30 priority government services every 100 days (cadence, no end date).
- Raise financial efficiency of government services by at least 20 percent by end of 2021.
- No per target owner is stated; the e-Government of Ajman leads initiatives and a joint executive team is to be formed by the higher committee for smart transformation. No baseline or measurement method is given.
