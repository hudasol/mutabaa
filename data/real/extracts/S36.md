# S36: Hamdan bin Mohammed launches Dubai Metaverse Strategy (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

* Launch announcement dated 18 July 2022, by Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed in line with directives of Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid.
* Aim: to turn Dubai into one of the world's top 10 metaverse economies. No target year is stated for this ranking aim.
* Jobs: support for more than 40,000 virtual jobs by 2030.
* The page mentions a UAE vision of raising blockchain companies to five times the present number; this is not a Dubai specific target and has no year.
* Focus areas: extended reality, digital twins, 5G and edge infrastructure, and sectors such as tourism, education, retail, remote work, healthcare and legal services.
* The page names no single implementing owner and no measurement source, and gives no baseline for the jobs figure.
