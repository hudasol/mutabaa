# S55: Sharjah Executive Council approves Sharjah's 2026-2028 digital strategy (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- The Sharjah Executive Council (chaired by Sheikh Sultan bin Mohammed bin Sultan Al Qasimi) approved a digital transformation strategy for the period 2026 to 2028, announced 25 Nov 2025.
- The strategy is said to contain "more than 50 initiatives"; they are not listed on the page.
- It sets up a "Higher Committee for Digital Integration" for unified oversight, coordination and decision making, and a "Digital Cooperation Charter" to clarify roles of government entities.
- Stated aims are qualitative: digitise services, improve institutional performance, build secure shared systems, strengthen cybersecurity and data privacy, align with national priorities.
- The page does not contain numeric KPIs, a baseline, a budget, named initiative owners, interim milestones or a measurement method. Only the 2026 to 2028 period is given as a date.
