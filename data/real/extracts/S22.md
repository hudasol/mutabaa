# S22: Cabinet meeting, 18 May 2026 (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Goal restated: to be the first government in the world to deploy Agentic AI across 50% of its services and operations (already in the ledger; not re-extracted).
- 80,000 federal employees are to be trained in Agentic AI across 5 occupational categories. No deadline is given for the training.
- Phase One federal services fall into 4 categories: citizens, residents, business sector and general public services.
- Each ministry or federal entity forms a team headed by its Minister or head of entity; teams set targets and assessment indicators, which are not quantified.
- Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan will lead and oversee the transformation journey.
- The National Policy for AI in healthcare is to expand digital and AI use across care types and to ensure security, ethics and data governance of AI applications in the health sector.
- The Cabinet approved drafting a federal health law covering health data governance, safety standards, liability and patient rights. No entity or date is named.
- The Digital Wellbeing Council was reconstituted under Sheikh Saif bin Zayed Al Nahyan.
