* Portal summary of the Dubai Universal Blueprint for AI, designed to accelerate adoption of artificial intelligence and to support the Dubai Economic Agenda D33.

* First phase of the annual plan lists five deliverables: an AI chief executive in each government entity, AI and Web3 incubators, an AI week in schools and universities, an AI commercial licence, and land for data centres.
