# S20: Digital Economy Strategy (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-11-03 via web-fetch extraction. Notes, not source text.

- Target: raise the digital economy share of GDP from 9.7% in 2022 to 19.4%, described as doubling the contribution within 10 years.
- More than 30 initiatives and programmes, 6 targeted sectors, and 5 new growth areas; none is named on the page.
- Goals without figures: strengthen the UAE position as a digital economy hub, define priorities for all sectors, and use a unified mechanism to measure growth periodically.
- A Council for Digital Economy supports implementation. The page links to the office of the Minister of State for AI, Digital Economy and Remote Work Applications but does not say it owns the strategy.
- The page gives no end year and no named measurement report.
