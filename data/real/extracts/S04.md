# S04 — DGE: Records landmark year under AI-native vision (published 8 January 2026) (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction of the DGE page. Notes, not source text.

- January 2025: DGE launched the Digital Strategy 2025-2027, backed by AED 13 billion; stated goal is the world's first AI-native government by 2027.
- The strategy targets 200+ AI solutions across government. The article does not report how many were deployed by end of 2025.
- 2025: 95% of public sector employees completed AI training.
- 2025: Chief Digital and AI Officers were appointed in every Abu Dhabi Government entity.
- 2025: AI Majalis created learning hubs for baseline AI skills.
- TAMM in 2025: 3.8 million users and more than 1,150 services; 95% of requests resolved through AI; over 1.9 million service cases completed via conversational AI; over 36 million customer visits eliminated annually; 90% of requests resolved within one day; 92.5% user satisfaction.
- TAMM AutoGov launched; it handles recurring needs such as licence renewals and medical appointments automatically.
- Sovereign cloud: partnerships with Microsoft and G42 expanded sovereign cloud capability; the Unified Government Data Centre launched with e&. Full sovereign cloud migration is a strategy target; the article does not report it as complete.
- Digitisation: the strategy targets 100% end-to-end digitisation of government processes; the article does not report progress against this target.
- The strategy includes a unified ERP system as a target; no completion is reported.
- Effortless Customer Experience Strategy (2025): based on more than 600,000 data points from customer feedback; 400,000 fewer service centre visits; 23% fewer steps in service journeys; 64% fewer customer requests.
- Projections, not achievements: AED 24 billion added to GDP and 5,000 new jobs.
