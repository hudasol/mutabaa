# S11 — UAE government portal: We the UAE 2031 vision (extraction notes)

Collected 2026-10-09 via web-fetch extraction of the UAE government portal page. Notes, not source text.

- Launched 22 November 2022 during the UAE Government Annual Meetings.
- Key national indicators: GDP to double from AED 1.49 trillion to AED 3 trillion; non-oil exports AED 800 billion; tourism contribution AED 450 billion; foreign trade AED 4 trillion.
- Rankings targeted: 1st globally for proactive legislation covering new economic sectors; top 10 for Human Development Index and healthcare quality; Emirati cities in the top 10 for quality of life; top 10 for attracting global talent; 1st on the safety index; top 10 on the Global Food Security Index; top three on the Global Cybersecurity Index.
- Four pillars: Forward Society; Forward Economy; Forward Diplomacy; Forward Ecosystem (improving government performance and infrastructure, including digital infrastructure, using modern technology).
- Owner: the page does not name a specific owner; all government entities, institutions and the private sector are to cooperate.
- The page does not state which index measures "proactive legislation".
