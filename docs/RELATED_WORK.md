## What was not found

No free tool was found that reads the wording of a government digital or AI target, applies named checks, and reports which check fails. No published check of such a score against later public reporting was found either. This was searched for, not proven absent. The two statements are therefore stated as "not found on 2026-11-03".

## Decisions that follow

1. The OGP verifiability yes or no is cited as precedent. Mutabaa does not integrate with it, because it is a reviewer judgement attached to OGP action plans only.
2. No existing free tool replaces the linter, the validity study, the definition card or the living ledger. They are built here.
3. Pages that were only seen as search results are not treated as read. They are cited as leads. If a claim in this repository comes to depend on one of them, the page will be read first and the row updated.
