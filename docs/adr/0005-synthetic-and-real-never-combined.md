# ADR 0005: Synthetic and real data are never combined

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
The Definition Lab needs a population of services and operations to show how much a headline percentage depends on its definition. Service inventories at entity level are not public. A synthetic registry makes the demonstration possible, but a reader who skims could take a synthetic figure for a statement about a real entity, and a number that mixed real and synthetic inputs could not be interpreted at all.

## Options considered
1. Estimate a real registry from public fragments and present it as approximate.
2. Use a synthetic registry and blend its results with the real ledger in summary figures.
3. Use a clearly labelled synthetic registry, keep it in its own files, and never combine it with real data in one number.

## Decision
We chose option 3. The registry is generated by `pipeline/synth.py` from a fixed seed and stored in `data/synthetic/registry.json`, apart from the real data in `data/real/`. In `pipeline/schemas.py` the field `is_synthetic` is `Literal[True]` on every entity, every item and the registry metadata, so an unlabelled record cannot load. Entity names are generic archetypes and never real names. The site shows a persistent badge on every view that uses the registry, and the home page leads with the real finding rather than with synthetic results.

## Consequences
- Results from the Definition Lab on this registry describe structure (how a definition moves a percentage), not where any real entity sits.
- A user can replace the synthetic registry with their own CSV, which is processed in the browser only.
- Some comparisons a reader might want, for example a real percentage under each definition, cannot be shown until a real registry is available. The pilot plan in `docs/PILOT.md` describes what that would take.
- The data statement for both datasets is `docs/DATA_STATEMENT.md`.
